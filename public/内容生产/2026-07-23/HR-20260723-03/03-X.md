# X 草稿

## 主线程

**1/5** AP's July 23 close: Tesla -14.5%, Alphabet -7.1%. Two earnings reports became one portfolio event.

**2/5** Example: $10k account, $2,500 in each stock. Tesla loss = $362.50. Alphabet loss = $177.50.

**3/5** Combined loss = $540, or 5.4% of the account. Do not simply add the percentages; each applies to a different position value.

**4/5** At $1,000 in each name, the same market moves produce a $216 combined loss, or 2.16% of the account. Exposure changes the outcome.

**5/5** These are closing moves, not exact gaps or intraday lows. The lesson is portfolio math, not a direction call.
