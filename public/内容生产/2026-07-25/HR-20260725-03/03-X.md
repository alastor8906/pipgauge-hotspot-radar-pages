# X 草稿

## 主线程

**1/5** USD/JPY above 163 makes intervention headlines tempting. But the controllable question is simpler: what would 100, 200 or 300 adverse pips cost your account?

**2/5** At 163.78, one standard lot is about $6.13/pip for a USD account. So 0.10 lot is roughly $0.61/pip; 1.00 lot is roughly $6.13/pip.

**3/5** A 100/200/300-pip adverse move at 1 lot is about $613/$1,225/$1,838. At 0.1 lot it is about $61/$123/$184. Same chart move, very different account impact.

**4/5** With a $10k account and a $100 loss budget, the theoretical size falls from about 0.16 lot at 100 pips to 0.08 at 200 and 0.05 at 300. Wider distance should mean smaller size.

**5/5** None of this predicts intervention. It is a stress test, and slippage can still make the real loss worse than the clean calculation.
