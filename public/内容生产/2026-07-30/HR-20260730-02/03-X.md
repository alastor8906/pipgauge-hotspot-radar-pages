# X thread draft

1/ The Fed held its target range at 3.50%–3.75% in a 9–3 vote. Three members preferred a 25bp hike. That is a news hook—not a EUR/USD direction signal.

2/ Fix the loss budget first. Assume a $10,000 USD account, $100 maximum planned loss, and EUR/USD at roughly $10/pip per standard lot.

3/ 25 pip stop → 0.40 lot.
50 pip stop → 0.20 lot.
100 pip stop → 0.10 lot.

4/ A wider stop should reduce size if the dollar-risk ceiling stays fixed.

5/ Spread is separate. At 1 pip, opening cost is about $4/$2/$1. At 3 pips, it is about $12/$6/$3. Commission and slippage still need their own lines.

6/ The vote changed the headline. It should not silently expand the account’s loss budget. Sources: Federal Reserve July 29 statement and AP. Educational example only.
