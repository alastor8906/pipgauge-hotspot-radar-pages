# X 草稿

## 主线程

**1/5** Before the FOMC decision, direction is uncertain. Dollar risk does not have to be. Example: $10k account, maximum planned loss $100, EUR/USD.

**2/5** At roughly $10/pip per standard lot: 25-pip stop = 0.40 lot. 50 pips = 0.20. 100 pips = 0.10. Same loss budget, different distance.

**3/5** If spread widens from 1 to 3 pips, opening cost at 0.40 lot rises from about $4 to $12. At 0.20 lot, $2 to $6. Commission is separate.

**4/5** Common mistake: widen the stop for the event but keep the original lot size. That gives the chart more room by silently giving the account more risk.

**5/5** The $100 is still a baseline, not a guaranteed fill. Slippage can make the realized loss worse. No FOMC forecast here, just risk that can be calculated before the headline.
