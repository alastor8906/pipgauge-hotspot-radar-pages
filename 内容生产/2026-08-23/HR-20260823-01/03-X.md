1/ Jackson Hole runs Aug. 27–29. The Fed calendar lists Kevin Warsh for an Aug. 28, 10:00 ET keynote. That is a schedule fact — not an EUR/USD forecast.

2/ The useful pre-event question is numerical: if the loss budget is fixed, does the position size shrink when the planned stop is wider?

3/ Example: USD 10,000 account; 1% planned price risk = USD 100. For EUR/USD in a USD account, one standard lot is approximately USD 10 per pip.

4/ Same USD 100 budget: 25 pips = 0.40 lot. 50 pips = 0.20 lot. 100 pips = 0.10 lot.

5/ Keep 0.40 lot and widen a planned stop from 25 to 100 pips, and planned price risk rises from about USD 100 to USD 400 before costs.

6/ A policy speech does not make a direction call. Spread, commission and slippage need a separate hypothetical stress test, and a stop is not a guaranteed fill.

7/ Sources: Kansas City Fed symposium page; Federal Reserve August calendar; Reuters event context. Educational example only, not financial advice.
