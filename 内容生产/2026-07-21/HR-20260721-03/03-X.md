# X 帖串

## 1/5

Ahead of CME's first expanded weekend window, “gold trades 24/7” did not mean every gold product had the same contract, hours or risk.

CME 1-Ounce Gold Futures and retail XAUUSD CFDs are different instruments.

## 2/5

Assume a broker defines 1.00 XAUUSD lot as 100 oz.

A $25 gold move becomes:

0.01 lot (1 oz) → $25  
0.10 lot (10 oz) → $250  
1.00 lot (100 oz) → $2,500

## 3/5

For a 1-Ounce futures contract, that same $25 price move is roughly $25 before costs.

Same reference price. Different exposure unit.

## 4/5

“Gold pip” can also be ambiguous.

Some platforms use $0.01. Others display $0.10 or points differently.

Contract ounces × dollar price move is often the cleaner comparison.

## 5/5

Before comparing gold products, check:

• venue  
• contract ounces  
• minimum size  
• trading hours  
• spread/commission/margin

Open 24/7 does not mean equally liquid 24/7.
