# X 帖串

## 1/5

One day before the July 23 ECB decision, the controllable risk was simple: widening a stop without resizing the position could quietly multiply account exposure.

Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk budget = $100.

## 2/5

For EUR/USD in a USD account:

25-pip stop → 0.40 lot  
50-pip stop → 0.20 lot  
100-pip stop → 0.10 lot

Different chart room. Same planned dollar loss.

## 3/5

Keep 0.40 lot while moving the stop from 25 to 100 pips, and the planned loss rises from about $100 to $400.

“More room” is not free.

## 4/5

Spread is a separate cost.

At 0.40 lot:

0.8-pip spread ≈ $3.20  
3.0-pip spread ≈ $12.00

Event conditions can change both spread and execution quality.

## 5/5

The controllable checklist:

1. Dollar risk budget  
2. Updated stop distance  
3. Resized position  
4. Spread assumption  
5. Slippage allowance

Predict less. Define the exposure first.
