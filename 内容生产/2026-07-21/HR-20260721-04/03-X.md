# X 帖串

## 1/5

With TSLA and GOOGL due to report the next day, two different tickers still formed one event-risk window.

Sizing each trade separately may miss the combined gap exposure.

## 2/5

Example: $10,000 account.

$2,000 TSLA position  
$2,000 GOOGL position

Total capital exposed to the same event window: $4,000.

## 3/5

If both positions gap against you:

5% each → -$200 combined (2% of account)  
10% each → -$400 (4%)  
15% each → -$600 (6%)

This is a stress test, not a forecast.

## 4/5

Why not rely only on stops?

An after-hours gap can skip the stop price. Planned stop risk and gap risk are different scenarios and should be measured separately.

## 5/5

Reverse the math:

Max combined loss: $200  
Stress gap: 10%

Maximum combined position value ≈ $2,000.

Different tickers do not automatically equal diversification.
