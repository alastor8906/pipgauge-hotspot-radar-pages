# X 帖串

## 1/5

An ECB headline can move EUR/USD, but it does not decide what the move costs your account.

A 50-pip move means very different things at 0.10, 0.50 and 1.00 lot.

## 2/5

For a USD account, one standard EUR/USD lot is commonly about $10 per pip.

50 pips at:

0.10 lot → about $50  
0.50 lot → about $250  
1.00 lot → about $500

## 3/5

Now assume a $10,000 account and a fixed $100 risk budget.

20-pip stop → about 0.50 lot  
50-pip stop → about 0.20 lot

Wider stop, smaller position.

## 4/5

The clean calculation is only a baseline.

Around a policy release, spread widening and slippage can make the realized loss larger than the planned stop amount.

## 5/5

The useful pre-event question is not only “What will the ECB do?”

It is: “If EUR/USD moves 50 pips against me, how many dollars does this position lose?”
