# X 帖串

## 1/5

“Gold trades 24/7” does not make every gold product the same.

CME 1-Ounce Gold Futures and a retail XAUUSD CFD can reference similar prices while carrying very different exposure.

## 2/5

Start with ounces.

CME 1OZ: 1 contract = 1 oz.

A common retail XAUUSD convention is 1.00 lot = 100 oz, but the broker specification must be checked.

## 3/5

For a $25 gold move:

CME 1OZ → about $25
XAUUSD 0.01 lot → about $25
XAUUSD 0.10 lot → about $250
XAUUSD 1.00 lot → about $2,500

Before costs.

## 4/5

Trading availability is not the same as constant liquidity.

Weekend depth, spreads, maintenance windows and broker schedules still belong in the risk check.

## 5/5

Before comparing two gold positions, record:

• venue
• ounces per contract or lot
• tick definition
• trading hours
• spread, commission and margin

The label “gold” is not a contract specification.
