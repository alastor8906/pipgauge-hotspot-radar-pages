# X 草稿

## 主线程

**1/5** CME single-stock futures move into production Sunday for the Monday trade date. The useful first question is not “which stock wins?” It is “what exposure does one contract create?”

**2/5** CME documents 100 shares for the standard contract. Notional = share price × 100. A hypothetical $50/$200/$500 stock creates $5k/$20k/$50k notional.

**3/5** A 1% underlying move would be about $50/$200/$500 per contract. That is scenario P&L, not a directional forecast.

**4/5** Notional, margin and maximum loss are different numbers. Margin is collateral; it does not erase exposure or cap loss. Broker requirements can also be higher.

**5/5** Verify the exact CME product, multiplier and broker availability, then calculate notional, stress P&L and total portfolio exposure. This thread uses only the officially documented standard contract.
