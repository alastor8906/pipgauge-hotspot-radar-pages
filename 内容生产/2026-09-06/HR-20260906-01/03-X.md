**2/4**

The separate mechanical question is point-value exposure:

quantity × planned point distance × point value

The formula makes an assumption visible. It does not tell anyone whether to open, close, or hold a CFD position.

**3/4**

Teaching example only:

0.50 contract × 30 planned points × hypothetical USD 1 per point = USD 15 before costs.

USD 1 is not asserted as FTMO’s or any broker’s actual point value. Symbol specs and account currency must be verified.

**4/4**

An early close does not predict direction, liquidity, spread, volatility, or a fill. Commission, financing, slippage, gaps, minimum size and firm rules can all change actual results.

Verify the firm’s own source timezone and your own symbol specification separately.
