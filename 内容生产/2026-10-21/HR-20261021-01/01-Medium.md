# FOREX.com Japan Adds Weekend US Index CFDs. Verify the Point Value Before the First Saturday Session

FOREX.com’s Japan business announced on October 21 that its US SP 500 and US Tech 100 CFDs will be available at the weekend. The announcement’s schedule lists a Saturday session beginning at 17:00 JST and running to 06:00 the following day, with a daily 06:00–07:00 maintenance break.

That is a product-and-time verification task first. It is not an S&P 500 or Nasdaq forecast, a reason to trade over the weekend, or evidence that another broker uses the same hours or contract terms.

## Confirm the exact product before doing any math

[Finance Magnates’ same-day report](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/forexcom-lets-japanese-clients-trade-us-stock-indices-on-weekends/) independently identifies the launch as a FOREX.com Japan offering for US SP 500 and US Tech 100 CFDs, with the Saturday session opening at 17:00 Tokyo time. [FX News Group also reported the launch](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/forex-com-launches-weekend-trading-for-us-sp-500-and-us-tech-100-cfds-in-japan/).

Those articles confirm that the announcement is current. The controlling source for product availability and hours remains [StoneX’s October 21 announcement](https://www.stonex.com/en-us/media-room/press-releases/forex-com-launches-the-first-weekend-trading-service-in-japan-for-us-sp-500-and-us-tech-100-cfds/). A reader must still check the exact account, symbol and platform Market Information Sheet.

FOREX.com’s [index FAQ](https://mc-5dc23f93-43d0-491b-9a0f-708421-cm.azurewebsites.net/en/help-and-support/indices/) says its 7-Day index products use alternative reference markets and other relevant information when traditional index futures are closed. It also says weekend spreads may be wider. That is a reason to verify the product details; it is not a quantified cost or execution forecast.

## The useful calculation stays variable

Once the reader has verified the account’s own contract specification, planned price exposure is:

`quantity × planned point distance × account-currency value per point`

| Input | Teaching value |
|---|---:|
| Quantity | 0.10 contract |
| Planned point distance | 40 points |
| Point value | reader-verified JPY per point |
| Planned price exposure | `0.10 × 40 × reader-verified JPY value` |

If a reader’s own product sheet says JPY 500 per point, that conditional arithmetic is JPY 2,000: `0.10 × 40 × 500`. JPY 500 is not a FOREX.com term, a live quote, or a universal US SP 500 or US Tech 100 value. It only shows how the formula works after the reader has checked their own specification.

This does not include spread, commission, financing, slippage, gaps, minimum size, margin calls, or an execution result. A weekend availability announcement cannot establish any of those inputs for an individual account.

## A short verification sequence

1. Check the live StoneX notice for the announced product and the Saturday 17:00 JST window.
2. Confirm the reader’s actual FOREX.com Japan account, exact 7-Day symbol and Market Information Sheet.
3. Record the quantity, planned point distance, account currency and point value from that specification.
4. Calculate the variable exposure and keep it separate from costs, availability and execution assumptions.
