2/ The useful tomorrow-before-results question: how much overnight-gap exposure is already carried in a share position?

3/ Teaching example: USD 10,000 account, eight shares, USD 218 reference anchor. The anchor is not a live quote, valuation view, or target.

4/ A 5% / 10% / 15% absolute move is about USD 87.20 / USD 174.40 / USD 261.60 for eight shares.

5/ Separate calculation: USD 100 planned loss ÷ USD 12 stop distance = 8.33 shares, so eight after rounding down. Planned stop risk is about USD 96 before fees.

6/ Those numbers answer different questions. A stop-based size does not guarantee an overnight result, because a gap can bypass a stop.
