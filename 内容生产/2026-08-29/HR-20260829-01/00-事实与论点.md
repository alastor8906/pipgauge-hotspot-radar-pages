| Planned stop | Approx. USD risk per 1.00 lot | Fixed-risk size before rounding | Rounded down |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 pips | USD 250 | 0.400 | 0.40 lot |
| 50 pips | USD 500 | 0.200 | 0.20 lot |
| 100 pips | USD 1,000 | 0.100 | 0.10 lot |
