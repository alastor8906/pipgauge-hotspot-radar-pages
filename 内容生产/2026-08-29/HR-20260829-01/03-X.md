1/ Jackson Hole is no longer a countdown. AP and Reuters reported the August 28 keynote. That is a dated fact, not an EUR/USD or Fed-policy call.

2/ A useful post-event question is mechanical: if planned USD risk stays fixed, how does EUR/USD size change when the planned stop changes?

3/ Teaching assumption only: EUR/USD in a USD account is about USD 10/pip per 1.00 standard lot. Verify the actual pair and account setup separately.

4/ USD 10,000 account; USD 100 maximum planned price risk; costs excluded.

5/ 25-pip stop: USD 250 at 1.00 lot → about 0.40 lot after rounding down.

6/ 50-pip stop: USD 500 at 1.00 lot → about 0.20 lot. 100-pip stop: USD 1,000 → about 0.10 lot.

7/ The table does not forecast a 25/50/100-pip move or a policy decision. It holds one stated dollar limit constant while the plan’s stop changes. A stop is not a guaranteed fill.

8/ Sources: Kansas City Fed; AP, Aug. 28; Reuters, Aug. 28. Educational example only, not financial advice.
