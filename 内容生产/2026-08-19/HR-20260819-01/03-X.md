1/ Australia’s July Labour Force data is scheduled for Aug. 20 at 11:30 a.m. AEST. The useful AUD/USD question before a scheduled release is not “which way?” It is “does my loss limit remain fixed?”

2/ Calendar context only: employment-change consensus is 15k versus 76.3k prior; unemployment-rate consensus is 4.5% versus 4.4% prior. These are not actual July results and do not forecast AUD/USD.

3/ Example: $10,000 USD account, 1% risk = $100. For AUD/USD in a USD account, one standard lot is approximately $10 per pip.

4/ Same $100 budget: 25-pip stop = 0.40 lot. 50-pip stop = 0.20 lot. 75-pip stop = about 0.13 lot. A wider stop requires a smaller size.

5/ Keep 0.40 lot and widen a stop from 25 to 75 pips, and planned price risk rises from $100 to $300 before costs. That is arithmetic, not a jobs-data forecast.

6/ Spread, commission and slippage need a separate stress case. A stop is not a guaranteed fill, and a calculator checks inputs rather than predicting a release or currency move.

7/ Sources: Australian Bureau of Statistics Labour Force release calendar; Westpac Economics August calendar. Educational example only, not financial advice.
