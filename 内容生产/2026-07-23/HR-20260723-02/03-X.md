## 主线程

**1/5** The ECB held its deposit rate at 2.25%. Immediate commentary said the decision itself did not produce a material market surprise.

**2/5** That does not make pre-event sizing pointless. It shows why an important calendar event is not a forecast of a large pip move.

**3/5** On a $10k account with a $100 risk budget, 25/50/100-pip stops imply about 0.40/0.20/0.10 lot if EUR/USD is roughly $10 per pip per standard lot.

**4/5** Those are loss-budget scenarios, not claims about the actual event range. A post-event range needs timestamped prices and a defined measurement window.

**5/5** Calendar importance and account impact are different numbers. Size the second one; do not guess it from the first.
