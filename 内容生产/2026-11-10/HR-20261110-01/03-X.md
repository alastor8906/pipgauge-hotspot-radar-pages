2/5 A useful post-release question can stay mechanical: if your maximum planned price risk is fixed, what happens to planned quantity when your chosen stop input gets wider?

3/5 Teaching only: USD 50 planned risk; EUR/USD in a USD account; approximate USD 10/pip per standard lot.

25 pips → 0.20 lot
50 pips → 0.10 lot
75 pips → about 0.06 lot, rounded down

4/5 Those 25/50/75-pip inputs are not CPI range estimates. They simply demonstrate: planned risk ÷ (planned stop × pip value). Actual pip value, contract size, account currency, costs and minimum size can differ.

5/5 A daily-loss ceiling is a separate account-budget check, not a guarantee a single trade fits or that a stop fills at its planned price. Verify the current rules and your inputs; do not turn a CPI headline into a direction call.
