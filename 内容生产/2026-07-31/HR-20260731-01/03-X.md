# X 草稿

## 主线程

**1/5** BOJ held its overnight rate around 1.0% in an 8–1 vote. One member proposed 1.25%, but that proposal was defeated. Those facts are a news hook—not a USD/JPY direction signal.

**2/5** Use 160.00 as a round historical reference. For a USD account, 1 standard lot is about $6.25/pip: 100,000 × 0.01 ÷ 160.

**3/5** At 1 lot, adverse moves of 50/100/300 pips are about $313/$625/$1,875. At 0.1 lot they are about $31/$63/$188.

**4/5** With a fixed $100 loss budget, the theoretical sizes are about 0.32 lot at 50 pips, 0.16 at 100 and 0.05 at 300 after rounding down. Wider distance means smaller size.

**5/5** This is a baseline, not a fill guarantee. Spreads and slippage can worsen the result. And a market-source intervention report is not the same as official MOF confirmation.
