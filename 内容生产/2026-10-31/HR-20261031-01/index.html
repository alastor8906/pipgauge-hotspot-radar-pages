FTMO’s US DST Reset: Verify the Maintenance Window and Daily-Loss Clock Before November 2

FTMO’s October 29 CFD update creates a current clock-checking task, not a market outlook. The useful question is not where a currency, index, or stock may move. It is which account display actually defines a daily-loss boundary when a weekend maintenance window and the US daylight-saving change appear together.

FTMO’s October 29 trading update lists maintenance for MT4, MT5, TradingView, and cTrader on Saturday, October 31, from 08:00 to 18:00 MetaTrader platform time, and on Sunday, November 1, from 07:00 to 10:00. It says trading on those platforms is disabled during the listed maintenance and access may be restricted.

The same update says the US daylight-saving change occurs on the platform at 09:00, moving back to 08:00 on November 1. From November 2, FTMO says the Prague (CET) and MetaTrader (GMT+2) relationship returns to its usual one-hour difference. FTMO also says Max Daily Loss is monitored in Prague time and points users to the Today’s Permitted Loss countdown in Account MetriX for the account-specific reset check.

The numbers provide a verification sequence. They do not calculate a position size by themselves, and they do not establish that a new daily risk budget is available.

Refresh the verification sequence

This refresh replaces the earlier October 24 maintenance / European DST note rather than creating another generic daily-loss post.

  1. Open the current FTMO October 29 update and confirm that its product, platform, dates and wording apply to the account being checked.
  2. Keep the official maintenance window separate from both the platform display and local clock.
  3. Before relying on any day boundary, inspect the live Account MetriX countdown for Today’s Permitted Loss.
  4. From November 2, use the one-hour relation only as FTMO’s current timing context; do not infer a local reset or account outcome from it.

After those checks, PipGauge’s Forex Session Clock can place reader-verified Prague, platform and local labels side by side. Its Daily Loss Limit tool can keep a reader-entered daily boundary separate from later risk planning. Position Size is only relevant after balance, open P&L, contract value, costs and a planned stop have been independently confirmed. These tools do not establish FTMO terms, diagnose maintenance, tell a reader when their account resets or guarantee compliance.

Prop Firm Compare’s change log independently records the expanded weekend-maintenance and US-DST wording. It is useful discovery context, but FTMO’s current update and the reader’s own account display remain the controlling checks.

The maintenance hook expires after 10:00 MetaTrader time on November 1. Do not use the US-DST headline after the November 2 check unless the live official update, applicable account and countdown have been rechecked.
