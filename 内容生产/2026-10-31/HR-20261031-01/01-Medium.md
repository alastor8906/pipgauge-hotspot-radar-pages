# FTMO’s US DST Reset: Verify the Maintenance Window and Daily-Loss Clock Before November 2

FTMO’s October 29 CFD update creates a current clock-checking task, not a market outlook. The useful question is not where a currency, index, or stock may move. It is which account display actually defines a daily-loss boundary when a weekend maintenance window and the US daylight-saving change appear together.

FTMO’s [October 29 trading update](https://ftmo.com/en/blog/trading-updates/trading-update-29-oct-2026/) lists maintenance for MT4, MT5, TradingView, and cTrader on Saturday, October 31, from 08:00 to 18:00 MetaTrader platform time, and on Sunday, November 1, from 07:00 to 10:00. It says trading on those platforms is disabled during the listed maintenance and access may be restricted.

The same update says the US daylight-saving change occurs on the platform at 09:00, moving back to 08:00 on November 1. From November 2, FTMO says the Prague (CET) and MetaTrader (GMT+2) relationship returns to its usual one-hour difference. FTMO also says Max Daily Loss is monitored in Prague time and points users to the `Today’s Permitted Loss` countdown in Account MetriX for the account-specific reset check.

## Four labels to verify, not a loss estimate

| Check | Current figure from FTMO’s update | What it does not tell you |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday maintenance | Oct 31, 08:00–18:00 platform time | what happens to a particular order or account |
| Sunday maintenance | Nov 1, 07:00–10:00 platform time | a universal platform-access outcome |
| US DST platform change | 09:00 back to 08:00 | your local reset time or a trading signal |
| From Nov 2 | 1-hour Prague/MetaTrader relation | a dollar-loss estimate, point value, or breach result |

The numbers provide a verification sequence. They do not calculate a position size by themselves, and they do not establish that a new daily risk budget is available.
