# X thread draft — awaiting human review

1/ The RBA schedules its Monetary Policy Decision Statement for September 29 at 2:30 pm AEST. That is a calendar fact, not an AUD/USD forecast.

2/ The useful pre-decision question is numerical: if the planned loss budget is fixed, does position size shrink when the planned stop is wider?

3/ Example: USD 10,000 account; 1% planned price risk = USD 100. AUD/USD in a USD account is approximately USD 10/pip per standard lot.

4/ Same USD 100 budget: 25-pip planned stop = 0.40 lot. 50 pips = 0.20 lot. 75 pips = about 0.13 lot before rounding.

5/ Keep 0.40 lot and move from 25 to 75 pips, and planned price risk changes from USD 100 to USD 300 before costs. Arithmetic, not an RBA view.

6/ Spread, commission and slippage need their own hypothetical stress case. A planned stop is not a guaranteed fill; a calculator checks inputs, not RBA policy or price direction.

7/ Sources: RBA calendar, Reuters and ABC decision-week coverage. Educational example only, not financial advice.
