## Post 2/5

Example daily P&L: -$500, +$2,000, -$300, +$1,000. Best Day = $2,000.

## Post 3/5

Positive-days-profit denominator: $2,000 + $1,000 = $3,000.

$2,000 / $3,000 = 66.7%.

## Post 4/5

Total-profit denominator: -$500 + $2,000 - $300 + $1,000 = $2,200.

$2,000 / $2,200 = 90.9%.
