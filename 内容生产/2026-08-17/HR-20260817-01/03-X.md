# X thread draft — awaiting human review

1/ FOMC minutes from the July 28–29 meeting are scheduled for Aug. 19 at 2:00 p.m. ET. Before a scheduled release, the useful EUR/USD question is not “where will it go?” It is “does my loss limit remain fixed?”

2/ Example: $10,000 USD account, 1% risk = $100. For EUR/USD in a USD account, one standard lot is approximately $10 per pip.

3/ Same $100 budget: 25-pip stop = 0.40 lot. 50-pip stop = 0.20 lot. 100-pip stop = 0.10 lot. A wider stop requires a smaller size.

4/ Keep 0.40 lot and widen a stop from 25 to 100 pips, and planned price risk rises from $100 to $400 before costs. That is arithmetic, not a Fed forecast.

5/ The July decision held rates steady and had three dissents. The minutes may add context, but they are not published yet. Do not turn an unknown document into a directional claim.

6/ Test commission, spread and slippage separately. A stop is not a guaranteed fill, and a calculator checks inputs rather than predicting EUR/USD.

7/ Sources: Federal Reserve August calendar and FOMC calendar; Associated Press week-ahead coverage. Educational example only, not financial advice.
