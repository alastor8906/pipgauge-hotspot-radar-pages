## Post 3/5

Teaching arithmetic only:

USD 1,000 day boundary − USD 200 editorial buffer = USD 800 maximum planned price risk before costs.

The USD 200 is not an FTMO rule or a cost estimate.

## Post 4/5

With a 40-point planned stop:

whole quantity = floor(800 ÷ (40 × your verified USD per point))

Verify the contract value in your own platform. Do not borrow it from another micro, mini, broker or platform. A planned stop is not a guaranteed exit.

## Post 5/5
