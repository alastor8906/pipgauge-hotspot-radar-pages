FundingPips Labs EXP 004 Has a No-Daily-Loss Option. The $750 Static-Loss Line Still Exists.

FundingPips' dated EXP 004 rulebook describes a limited 25K Labs account with two daily-loss configurations. The headline can be easy to misread: one option lists no daily loss. That does not mean the account has no loss boundary.

The official page lists a 3% static maximum loss for both configurations. On the stated USD 25,000 account, the rule-level arithmetic is:

3% × USD 25,000 = USD 750

For the alternative configuration, the page lists a 2% daily-loss line of USD 500 alongside the same USD 750 static maximum loss. These are separate constraints, not interchangeable trading allowances.

The calculation question worth checking

Before turning an account-rule headline into any planned-risk number, first confirm the current official EXP 004 page, the selected option, whether the account is in Evaluation or Master, and the applicable contract specification.

Then keep the rule boundary separate from reader-entered trade inputs. A USD 750 static maximum-loss amount is not a per-trade risk cap, a position-size instruction, an expected loss, or a statement about a stop order being filled. Costs, open P&L, commissions, gaps and platform terms can matter independently.

PipGauge's Challenge Simulator and Daily Loss Limit tools can help organise reader-entered risk-budget inputs after the rule is confirmed. PositionMath's Prop Firm Simulator can be a secondary input check. Neither calculator determines FundingPips eligibility, interprets the current rulebook, recommends an option, or predicts a trading result.

The limited-run framing should be retired if the official page changes, the product is no longer available, the rules/prices/options change, or the reader is not looking at this exact EXP 004 configuration.
