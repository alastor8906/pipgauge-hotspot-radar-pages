FTMO’s October 24 Maintenance: Recheck the Daily-Loss Clock Before the DST Week

FTMO’s latest CFD trading update creates a timing check, not a market outlook. Before the Europe/US daylight-saving transition week, the useful question is not where a currency, index, or stock may move. It is which account display actually defines the daily-loss boundary when a scheduled platform interruption and different clocks appear in the same week.

FTMO’s October 22 trading update says MT4, MT5, TradingView, and cTrader are scheduled for maintenance on Saturday, October 24, from 08:00 to 12:00 MetaTrader platform time (GMT+3). During that window, it says trading is disabled and access may be restricted.

The same dated update says that from October 26 through November 1, Prague midnight and MetaTrader midnight are two hours apart, rather than the usual one hour. FTMO says Max Daily Loss is monitored on Prague time and points users to the countdown shown under Today’s permitted loss in Account MetriX for the account-specific reset check.

Three timing labels, not a loss estimate

What to verifyCurrent number in FTMO’s updateWhat it does not tell you
Saturday maintenanceOct 24, 08:00–12:00 GMT+3whether a particular order, stop, or account will have a particular outcome
Usual Prague/MetaTrader midnight relationship1 houra universal broker or local reset time
Oct 26–Nov 1 relationship2 hoursa dollar-loss estimate, point value, or trading signal

Those figures are a prompt to check the right display. They do not produce a trade instruction, and they do not convert into a position-size number by themselves.

A refresh to the verification sequence

This update refreshes the earlier October 15 DST note rather than creating a second generic daily-loss article. The October 22 fact adds a concrete Saturday checkpoint and current affected-platform context. The verification sequence remains deliberately narrow:

  1. Open the current FTMO October 22 update and confirm that its product, platform, dates, and wording apply to the account you are checking.
  2. Before relying on a date boundary, inspect the live Account MetriX countdown for Today’s permitted loss.
  3. Keep Prague time, the platform display, and your local clock as separate labels. During October 26–November 1, do not treat a platform date change as proof that the daily-loss day has reset.
  4. Keep the time check separate from planned risk. A daily-loss boundary is not automatically available risk for a new position, and a reset does not erase open P&L, costs, contract terms, or other account conditions.

After those checks, PipGauge’s Forex Session Clock can help present reader-verified clock labels side by side. Its Daily Loss Limit tool can keep a reader-entered day boundary separate from later risk planning. Position Size is only relevant after the reader has separately confirmed balance, open P&L, contract value, and a planned stop. None of those tools establishes FTMO’s terms, diagnoses maintenance, tells a reader when a reset occurs, or guarantees compliance.

FindBestProp’s October 23 rule check independently records the new update and timing sequence, but FTMO’s current update and the reader’s own account display remain the controlling sources. A public Reddit discussion is only evidence that access and daily-loss wording are being discussed; it is not evidence of what happened to any account.

This maintenance-specific framing expires when the Saturday window begins at 08:00 GMT+3 on October 24. The DST wording must be retired after November 1 or immediately if FTMO changes the dates, platform scope, reset language, applicable product, or the live Account MetriX countdown conflicts with the draft.
