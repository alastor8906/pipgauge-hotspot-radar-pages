**Post 1/4**

Broadcom is scheduled to report after Wednesday’s close. Before turning that into a price call, there is a simpler question: what does an AVGO position mean in dollars if the price moves materially after results?

**Post 2/4**

Teaching example, not a live quote or forecast:

USD 10,000 account
10 AVGO shares
USD 400 per-share anchor

That is USD 4,000 of one-stock exposure: 40% of the account.

**Post 3/4**

Under symmetric hypothetical absolute changes:

5% = USD 200 on 10 shares
10% = USD 400
15% = USD 600

Those are stress checks—not expected moves, probabilities, or a view on earnings direction.

**Post 4/4**

Normal planned sizing is different from gap exposure:

USD 100 planned risk ÷ USD 20 planned stop = 5 shares before fees.

A stop can guide normal sizing, but it does not guarantee an overnight gap exit. Check the arithmetic first; don’t turn a scheduled report into a prediction.
