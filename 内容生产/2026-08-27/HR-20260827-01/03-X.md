2/ The useful result-day question: what dollar exposure does a stated share position represent after a reported move?

3/ Teaching example: USD 10,000 account, eight shares, USD 218 reference anchor. The anchor is not a live quote, target, or valuation claim.

4/ A reported approximately 7% absolute move = about USD 122.08 for eight shares. Symmetric 5% / 10% stress checks = USD 87.20 / USD 174.40.

5/ Separate math: USD 100 planned loss ÷ USD 12 stop distance = 8.33 shares, so eight after rounding down. Planned-stop risk is about USD 96 before fees.

6/ These are different questions. A holding-exposure table is not a forecast. A planned stop is not a guarantee of an exit after a gap.

7/ Sources: NVIDIA investor release; AP; Axios, Aug. 27. Educational example only, not financial advice.
