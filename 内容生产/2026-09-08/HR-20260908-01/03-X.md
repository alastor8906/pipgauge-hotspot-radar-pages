## Post 1/5

FTMO Futures beta has created a simple rule-reading question: a daily-loss limit is not a per-trade position-size target. Before sizing anything, separate the rule ceiling from the risk you plan to allocate. No futures-direction view required.

## Post 2/5

For a 50K Pro Evaluation, FTMO’s current rules list a USD 1,000 Daily Loss Limit and define it with equity, including open P&L and commissions. Recheck the official rules for the plan and phase you actually have; this is not a universal prop rule.

## Post 3/5

Teaching arithmetic only:

USD 1,000 day ceiling − USD 200 buffer = USD 800 maximum planned price risk before costs.

The USD 200 is an editorial example, not an FTMO instruction or an estimate of execution costs.

## Post 4/5

If the planned stop is 40 points, use your own verified contract value:

whole quantity = floor(800 ÷ (40 × USD per point))

Do not borrow a point value from another micro/mini, broker or platform. A planned stop is not a guaranteed exit.

## Post 5/5

Useful sequence: read the current rule → verify contract specs → reserve a buffer → calculate planned price risk. This does not recommend FTMO, any futures position or any account. Recalculate when rules, balance, open P&L or costs change.
