## 主线程

**1/5** The Bank of England held Bank Rate at 3.75%. The new number is the vote: 6–3, versus 7–2 in June. Three members preferred an immediate 25bp increase to 4%.

**2/5** That is evidence of a more divided committee. It is not evidence that GBP/USD must move in one direction.

**3/5** With the common USD-account assumption of about $10/pip per standard GBP/USD lot, a 25/50/100-pip move equals roughly $250/$500/$1,000 at 1.00 lot.

**4/5** To cap planned risk at $100: 25-pip stop ≈ 0.40 lot; 50 pips ≈ 0.20; 100 pips ≈ 0.10. Wider risk distance means smaller size.

**5/5** Then add the live spread and allow for slippage. The MPC vote is policy information; pip value, lot size and execution determine the account result.
