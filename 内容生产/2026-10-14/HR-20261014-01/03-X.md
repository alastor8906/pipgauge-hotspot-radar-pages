# FTMO 1-Step consistency is live for the new cohort. The useful question is order date + denominator, not market direction. 🧵

1/ FTMO’s current 1-Step objectives page says orders created on/after 12 Oct use Consistency: Best Day must be no more than 50% of total closed profit, including losing days. Earlier orders retain the legacy Best Day denominator.

2/ Official example: +$6,000, -$2,000, +$4,000, +$2,000. Total profit = $10,000. Best Day = $6,000. Ratio = 60%.

3/ If the confirmed threshold is 50% and Best Day remains $6,000, the required total profit is $12,000: $6,000 ÷ 0.50. That is arithmetic, not a trade plan or a pass/fail claim.

4/ Verify product, order/merge date, current official percentage, and closed-P&L definition before calculating. A correct ratio applied to the wrong cohort is still the wrong answer.

5/ The FTMO page says this is not a breach condition; it requires continued profit until the ratio qualifies. No account recommendation, no market call, no payout prediction.
